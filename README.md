# DebtFree AI — Free Debt Payoff Planner (PWA)

**[debtpayoffplanner.xyz](https://debtpayoffplanner.xyz)** — a free, installable web app that builds a real amortization schedule for your debt snowball or avalanche plan.

## What it does

- **Snowball & avalanche engines**: month-by-month payoff schedules computed with real amortization math (credit cards compound daily at APR/365; installment loans monthly)
- **One fixed number**: enter your debts and monthly budget, see the exact month you go debt-free
- **WhatsApp reminders**: gentle nudges when a payment is due
- **Works offline**: installable PWA — no app store needed, installs from the browser
- **No signup, no bank login, no card required** — your balances stay on your device

## Install (10 seconds)

1. Open [debtpayoffplanner.xyz](https://debtpayoffplanner.xyz) in Chrome, Edge, or Safari
2. Tap **Install app** (or Share → Add to Home Screen on iOS)
3. Done — it works offline from your home screen
